✗ Flat, static 2-tab lists (A Building vs Common) with no multi-project clustering.
✗ Only 4 rudimentary statuses ('Pending', 'In Progress', 'Done', 'On Hold').

✓ Mandatory Blocker reason on hold, and mandatory resolution audit trail upon unblocking.
State Machine Specification

The 10-State Formal Workflow Lifecycle

1. Draft

Initial idea, scope formulation.

→ To: Planned
2. Planned

Scope, deadline & budget set.

→ To: Ready
3. Ready

Prerequisites & vendor assigned.

→ To: In Progress
4. In Progress

Underway on site by vendor.

→ To: In Review / Blocked
5. Blocked

Impediment (Reason required).

→ To: In Progress
6. In Review

Submitted for Committee signoff.

→ To: Approved / Rejected
7. Approved

Approved by Approver role.

→ To: Completed
8. Rejected

Returned for rework with notes.

→ To: In Progress
9. Completed

Quality checks passed & closed.

✓ Final Closed State
10. Cancelled

De-scoped or superseded.

Terminated with notes
Access Governance

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) & Gates

Executive Approvers
Chairman, Secretary, Treasurer, Admin
• Exclusive authority to advance tasks from 'In Review' to 'Approved' or 'Rejected'.
• Final signoff on project completion and contractor invoice release.
• Privilege to reopen closed or cancelled items with recorded audit rationale.
Operational Leads
Estate Manager, Lead Members & Staff
• Create and schedule tasks under committee initiatives.
• Transition tasks through 'Draft', 'Planned', 'Ready', and 'In Progress'.
• Mandated to submit tasks to 'In Review' with proof of completion & test certificates.
• Forbidden from self-approving work, enforcing segregation of duties.
Society Residents
Wing A Flat Owners & Tenants
• Read-only transparency into society ongoing initiatives and modernization progress.
• Real-time visibility into infrastructure punchlists, solar status, and water tank waterproofing.
• Zero-write access preventing unauthorized status manipulation.
User Experience

UI/UX Implementation: 5 Specialized Views

1. Executive Overview Dashboard

High-level KPIs: Active Projects, Completion Velocity %, Blocked Impediments, Pending Approvals, Overdue Items, and Total Budget vs Actual Spend.

2. Interactive Kanban Board

Phase-based lanes (Planning, Ready, In Progress, Blocked, Review, Completed) with 1-click status advances, priority badges, and overdue chips.

3. Comprehensive List & Table View

Data-dense grid with multi-column sorting, multi-attribute filtering (Project, Priority, Status, Assignee), instant search, and CSV export.

4. Committee Approval Desk

Dedicated executive queue filtering tasks in 'In Review' state. Approvers inspect completion notes and execute 1-click Approve or Reject.

5. Task Detail & Action Drawer

Slide-over panel containing technical scope, quality acceptance checklist, blocker risk log, cost breakdown, and chronological audit history.
